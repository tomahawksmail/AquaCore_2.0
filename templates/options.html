CPU cooler

CPU fan · PH8 Turns ON above the ON threshold; OFF at or below the OFF threshold. Temperatures between retain the previous state.

Keep at least 3°C between thresholds. If CPU temperature cannot be read, the relay worker requests fan ON. Fan control uses CPU temperature, not aquarium temperature.
